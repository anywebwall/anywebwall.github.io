The Tech Behind Zero-Lag Interactivity

The biggest problem with most interactive desktop backgrounds is that they consume massive processing power. Calculating fluid dynamics and mouse tracking on a high-resolution display usually requires a heavy web framework, causing FPS drops during gaming. At BSR Studios, we bypassed this issue by utilizing the AnyWebWall native C++ and OpenGL engine.

This cursor trail effect calculates its physics directly on your GPU. The result is a stunning visual experience that runs with an absolute 0% CPU usage and strictly consumes around ~20MB of RAM. Whether you are using a top-tier gaming rig or a low-end laptop with just 2GB of RAM, your system will remain fast, cool, and 100% lag-free.

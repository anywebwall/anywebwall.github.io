How to Make Your PC Look Like a Hacker Setup

Many users search for ways to customize their dull computer screens into something that looks like it belongs to a cyber-security expert. Achieving this aesthetic is actually quite simple and doesn't require any risky third-party tweaks. Here is a quick guide on how to make your PC look like a hacker setup easily on Windows 10 and 11.

Step 1: Install the Hacker Live Wallpaper

The core of any tech setup is the background. Static images are boring. By using the Hacker Window .aww file available on this page, you get moving code, scrolling text, and digital terminal interfaces running smoothly behind your icons.

  1. Download the free AnyWebWall Engine.
  2. Download the Hacker Window theme from the download section below.
  3. Launch AnyWebWall, click "Add Wallpaper", and select your downloaded file.

Step 2: Hide Your Desktop Icons

Real hackers don't have cluttered desktops filled with game shortcuts and folders. To get a clean look, simply right-click anywhere on your empty desktop, hover over View, and uncheck Show desktop icons. Your files are safe and accessible through the File Explorer, and your screen will now show the uninterrupted hacker animation!

Step 3: Auto-Hide Taskbar & Enable Dark Mode

To complete the immersive cyberpunk aesthetic, you should enable Windows Dark Mode and hide your taskbar so the code animation covers the entire screen edge-to-edge. Go to Windows Settings > Personalization > Colors and set your default app mode to Dark. Then go to Taskbar settings and turn on Automatically hide the taskbar.

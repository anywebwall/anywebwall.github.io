Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will a moving sky background drain my laptop battery?
Because the Parallax Sky script relies on optimized hardware-accelerated rendering rather than continuously decoding a high-resolution video file, it requires a minimal energy footprint. Furthermore, our engine automatically pauses rendering when full-screen apps are open, significantly saving battery life.

How do I interact with the rocket?
Once you apply the `.aww` file using the AnyWebWall engine, simply move your mouse cursor across your desktop. The physics engine dynamically calculates drag and velocity, causing the rocket companion to seamlessly fly toward your pointer.
